Logic Design Lab is Michelangelo Carrozzo’s independent laboratory. Since 2000, it has developed RF hardware, analog audio and FPGA logic for professional audio manufacturers.

Digital wireless microphones

System architecture, complete TX/RX hardware, wideband PCB antennas, audio circuits and the original radio protocol with FPGA clock recovery.

470–870 MHz · 126 dB audio dynamic range · AES encryption

RF & implementation notes

Receiver sensitivity of −97.4 dBm measured during development. The link was developed for crowded stages and interference-heavy RF conditions.

The original FPGA logic was designed in schematic capture, then migrated to VHDL under Michelangelo’s supervision. Specialist colleagues implemented DSP firmware, including the audio codec, from his protocol and hardware specifications.

Before Logic Design Lab

Radio, valves

From 1995 to 2000, Michelangelo was Editorial Director and electronics designer at Nuova Elettronica: PLL radios, vector impedance meters, counters and valve amplifiers, built as kits and explained to readers.

He wrote the articles with Giuseppe Montuschi, who taught him how to make difficult electronics understandable.
