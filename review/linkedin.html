About

I design RF and wireless audio systems. I run Logic Design Lab, my independent laboratory in Bologna, founded in 2000.

My designs have gone into products for RCF, dBTechnologies, beyerdynamic and DPA Microphones. I design the transmitter and receiver hardware, the radio protocol and the FPGA logic that recovers the data and clock. The work ranges from wideband PCB antennas and switched RF filters to low-noise audio circuits.

I also take the hardware into production: test benches, calibration procedures and time on the factory floor training operators. This has meant extended stays in China, as well as production work in Germany and Italy.

Before the lab, I designed electronics and was Editorial Director at Nuova Elettronica. PLL radios, impedance meters, TTL/CMOS logic and valve amplifiers were everyday work. Giuseppe Montuschi taught me how to explain it to readers.

Radio is still my main interest.

Independent RF and audio laboratory. System architecture, TX/RX hardware, analog audio, radio protocols and FPGA design.

  • dBTechnologies — DWS800 / RS16000 Touring Rack: 400 MHz digital wireless platform, radio protocol and FPGA clock recovery. Production transfer in China; the later RS16000 added PC control and Ethernet configuration.
  • beyerdynamic — TG1000 digital and TG500 analog wireless systems. RF/audio hardware; TG1000 radio protocol and FPGA clock recovery. Prototype validation and production support in Germany and Italy.
  • RCF — Forum conference hardware for up to 250 consoles, with encrypted UHF simultaneous interpretation. Coordinated the software/firmware team.
  • DPA Microphones — System architecture, TX/RX hardware and original protocol/FPGA clock recovery for a professional digital wireless platform. Supported production transfer and test engineering.

I implemented the original FPGA designs in schematic capture and supervised the later VHDL migration. Specialist colleagues implemented DSP firmware and the audio codec from my specifications. My embedded work includes C peripheral drivers and low-level STM32 debugging.

Current independent laboratory collaboration with Wisycom and DPA.
